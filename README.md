# 📉 CFD Trading

### What is CFD trading?

CFD, or Contract for Difference, trading involves speculating on the rising or falling prices of financial markets without owning the underlying asset. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

### How does leverage work?

Leverage allows traders to control a larger market position with a smaller amount of capital. However, leverage can significantly increase both potential gains and potential losses.

### What are the benefits of CFD trading?

CFDs provide traders with the ability to speculate on price movements across various financial markets without directly owning the underlying asset.

### Can I trade CFDs on cryptocurrencies?

Cryptocurrency CFD availability depends on the instruments offered through the trading platform.
